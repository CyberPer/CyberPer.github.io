Atomvoice

A personal voice assistant for the owner's own car. Not affiliated with Tesla or xAI.

Privacy

  • Atomvoice is used privately by its developer, for one vehicle.
  • Car access tokens and keys are stored only on the owner's phone and computer.
  • Voice and conversation text are sent to xAI to understand requests. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • If the owner connects Spotify, Atomvoice uses Spotify's Web API only to see and control playback on the owner's own account. Spotify sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's phone.
  • No data is collected through this website.
